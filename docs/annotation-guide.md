# Annotation protocol (draft)

**Not yet used with human annotators.** The current dataset is wholly synthetic.

## For each future source passage, record

1. Stable source identifier, creator, title, date, original language, publicly verifiable citation and reuse/license terms.
2. Exact excerpt with no silently altered words; provide a translation attribution where applicable.
3. Historical and cultural setting with independent documentary support.
4. Plausible readings with explicit quoted textual evidence, documented ambiguity and possible alternative readings.
5. Retrieval notes with source, publication date, credibility, relevance, truth-status, and the exact assertions supported or contradicted.
6. At least two independent annotations, separate confidence scores, disagreement and adjudication records.
7. Potential harms or stereotype risks from interpreting cultural material with unreliable evidence.

A culturally specific claim must be source-backed. Avoid inventing cultural traditions and treating them as fact. Evaluate multiple defensible interpretations with expert oversight rather than a forced one-label gold standard.

## Reviewer scoring proposal

Rate interpretive plausibility, fidelity to source passage, factual treatment of contextual notes, citation support, recognition of ambiguity and calibration. Apply the same rubric across retrieval conditions, blinded to the condition wherever possible. Log disagreements rather than discarding them.
